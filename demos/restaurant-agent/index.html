    How the production agent works

    The same conversation, with a language model and the restaurant's own system behind it.

    One message, step by step

    1. The customer's WhatsApp message reaches the agent through a webhook.
    2. The model reads it with the conversation so far and decides what is needed: the menu, a price, a new order or an order's status.
    3. Prices and totals come from the menu data in code, never from the model's own arithmetic.
    4. Nothing reaches the kitchen until the customer confirms the summary. Then the order is created and shows up on the kitchen screen.

    What this page shows

    • Quantities and items are read from what you type, including "شيل" to remove and "الغي" to cancel.
    • The kitchen ticket always matches the confirmed basket, and the status moves New, Cooking, Out for delivery, Delivered.
    • Every reply carries an English line under the Arabic.
