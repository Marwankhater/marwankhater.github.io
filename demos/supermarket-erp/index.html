Supermarket chain ERP on Odoo 19

HR, attendance and payroll for every branch of a supermarket chain, built as custom Odoo 19 modules and live in production. Three parts of it you can try: the general manager's morning brief, biometric attendance, and the Egyptian payroll calculation.

Interactive demo with sample data. The real system runs at a client, so its data stays private. There is no AI model in this one: the brief and its alerts come from fixed, tested rules, because the owner decided that no employee data leaves the server.

      Who bears the income tax
      • 16 custom Odoo 19 modulesEmployees and branches, attendance, leave, payroll, travel allowances, expense approval, recruitment, the brief, reports and dashboards. No Odoo core file and no third-party module is modified; everything extends through inheritance.
      • 4,000+ automated testsPayroll figures, the attendance rules, the brief's alerts and who can see what. A branch manager seeing another branch's data is a failing test.
      • Fingerprint attendance agentA small Python program on each branch PC, run as a scheduled task. It reads the devices and sends the punches to Odoo over HTTPS. Updates are signed.
      • POS sales pipelineSales come in from the point-of-sale database (SQL Server) to PostgreSQL every day, and each day's totals are reconciled against the source before anyone reports on them.
      • Competitor price collectorRuns every night and stores competitors' shelf prices next to the chain's own, for the commercial team.
      • DeploymentDocker, Nginx and fail2ban on the server, a staging copy for every release, and a CI gate that runs the tests before anything ships.
      • Payroll law in one placeIncome tax brackets of Law 7/2024 with the withdrawal of lower bands above EGP 600,000, the EGP 20,000 personal exemption, and social insurance at 11% and 18.75% within the yearly wage bounds. The bounds and rates are HR settings, so next year's change is data entry.
      • From requirements to go-liveBuilt with the client's HR and finance teams: their rules written down as specs, their data migrated, and each module released only after they checked it.
