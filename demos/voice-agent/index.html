    How the production agent works

    A phone call, handled end to end while the caller waits.

    One call, step by step

    1. The call is answered by the voice agent. Speech is turned into text as the caller talks.
    2. The model decides what the caller needs and asks for what is missing, like the order number.
    3. The order is looked up in the business's system during the call, and the answer is read back in Egyptian Arabic.
    4. Every call is logged with its topic and result, and anything the agent cannot handle goes to a person.
