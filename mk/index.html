What is Odoo, and why we build on it

Odoo is business management software used by companies around the world. We build on its open edition, Odoo Community, which has no license fee per user. We set it up around your branches, products and reports, and move your data in from Excel or your old system.

No license fee per userAdd staff and branches without paying a license for each new account.
Your data stays with youIt runs on a server in your company or on online hosting you choose, in your own country if you prefer.
Made for how you workScreens, reports, approvals and printouts changed to match your company, in Arabic and English.
Grows with youStart with sales and stock, then add accounting, manufacturing, HR and payroll when you're ready.
